# Farooq Ahmad Trading Company

A Flutter-based business management application designed to simplify daily operations for trading businesses. The app combines the power of Firebase Firestore for cloud sync and ObjectBox for local offline data storage, ensuring smooth performance even without an internet connection. It is tailored for users with minimal technical expertise while supporting high-volume data handling for professional use.

Key Features:

Customer Management – Maintain customer profiles with contact details and track balances automatically. Balances are color-coded: green for receivables and red for payables.

Sales & Purchases – Record transactions with itemized entries. Stock levels and account balances update in real time.

Payments & Ledgers – Accept cash or bank payments, with separate ledger tracking. Each entry includes date, method, and updated balances.

Expense Tracking – Log and categorize business expenses, which are automatically reflected in financial reports.

Reports & Analytics – Generate real-time reports for sales, purchases, expenses, profit & loss, and cash flow.

Stock Management – Keep track of product inventory with automatic updates on sales or purchases.

Database Structure:
ObjectBox for fast local data access and offline functionality.

Key collections include:

Customer, Item, Sale, Purchase, Expense, and Bank/Investment.

Tech Stack:

Frontend: Flutter (Dart)

Backend/Database: ObjectBox

Farooq Ahmad Trading helps businesses manage customers, track transactions, monitor stock, and analyze performance—all from a single, easy-to-use platform.
